## Questions for each review

- Can a first-time viewer explain the pain and outcome after the opening?
- Is the selected mode obvious? Is the next action visible without explanation?
- Does every narration claim have matching evidence in that shot?
- Is Qloo's actual contribution visible and distinct from catalog/LLM/checker roles?
- Does the revision tell a meaningful story, with restrictions preserved?
- Can we read critical UI at ordinary playback size? Do pointer/caption overlays obscure it?
- Is a preview, live saved result, illustration or simulated failure labelled accurately?
- Are pauses deliberate? Are any time cuts disclosed? Is narration natural and untreated?
- Have credentials, real personal data and private logs been excluded?

## Before any final release

- Confirm current official event rules and runtime; this package's 150-second target is provisional.
- Approve the script and a short CareTable narration audition before full paid generation.
- Resolve live voice or remove/qualify spoken-conversation claims. Never dub simulated success over a disconnected session.
- Capture fresh live integration and saved-state evidence against the intended build.
- Match captions to approved narration; validate shot ranges and actual encoded timings.
- Probe and fully decode MP4, check audio levels and peaks where audio exists, inspect opening/closing and all chapters.
- Owner watches and listens end-to-end; record approval separately from automated checks.
- Save editable sources, stems, captions, timeline, hashes and verification.
- Host and submit only as separate, explicitly completed steps.

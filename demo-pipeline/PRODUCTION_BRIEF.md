# CareTable production brief v0.1

## Intent and limits

Audience: hackathon judges and first-time users. Human focus: older adults, caregivers, partners and families. Demonstration uses one clearly fictional older-adult table as its narrative spine, with a brief view of household options. Do not imply clinical suitability or inferred health conditions.

Creative target: about 150 seconds, 16:9, readable 1280×720 minimum. **This is an editorial target, not a confirmed Qloo event limit.** Before final scripting, check the event's current official duration, deadline/timezone, required integrations, AI/media disclosures, hosting and repository access rules. No current-rule research was claimed during this local reference adaptation.

Proposed hook: “A meal can fit the checklist and still miss the person.” The product promise is familiar tastes plus explicit practical needs, followed by an explainable, revisable plan. Closing: “CareTable. A little more care at every meal.” These are copy proposals, not claims of measured impact.

## Build the story around proof

| Beat | Show | Claim boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Human stake | Owned food imagery, then editable fictional profile | Illustration, no testimonial or patient data |
| Personal needs | Favourites vs dislikes vs specific exclusions | Separate each person's inputs; no health inference |
| Grounding | A real saved live plan's Qloo entities, IDs, timestamp and separate recipe mapping | Qloo supplies taste evidence, not recipes or medical clearance |
| Reasoning | Original catalog recipe, recipients, ingredient/check panel | Deterministic listed checks; unknowns remain unknown |
| Change | A real targeted revision with old/new meal and preserved remaining slots | Only describe faster if the measured recipe time is lower; a shortage is an honest outcome |
| Continuity | Saved plan/history and refresh recovery | Use development evidence until the exact hosted release is checked |
| Voice | Default mode explanation and explicit Start voice control | Session creation/typed notes do not establish successful spoken audio; no fabricated waveform/captions |

Live voice is not a prerequisite for R0. Do not stage a successful spoken exchange until transport actually works. A separate labelled voice QA reel can be added when verified. The default mode can appear briefly before switching to User Mode for the reliable walkthrough.

## Adapted production method

1. Freeze a script version and claim/evidence ledger. Choose one outcome, not a tour of every setting.
2. Rehearse with synthetic state in a separate browser context. Record source media and event wall times; inspect actual video timestamps before editing.
3. Establish each screen, then move deliberately to the relevant controls. Avoid frantic scrolling and unreadable full-page shrinks. Keep safe space for captions.
4. R0 uses real browser interactions and local preview only. Keep its local-preview label visible. Failed takes retain failure status and must not be renamed successful.
5. R1 captures live evidence in separate takes. Shorten visible waits only with an explicit time-cut cue; never replace a provider response with synthetic success.
6. Approve narration before paid generation. Keep original voice bytes and script hash. Do not stretch speech or reuse Tonight Together audio. No music is needed for the first rehearsal.
7. Align speech to real word timestamps, then attach picture selections to the spoken beats. Retain a source-shot map. Do not assume action wall times equal media PTS.
8. Export H.264/yuv420p MP4 with faststart; use consistent 24 or 30 fps and, when audio exists, explicit 48kHz AAC. Never invent an audio stream to call a silent workprint narrated. Normalize only after listening; assess levels on the export.
9. Validate every source range, full decode, durations, captions, frame readability and owner watch/listen review. Save hashes and a manifest. Upload and submission are separate authorized steps.

## What to keep out of the main film

Production account provisioning, password-reset screens, admin configuration and the theme picker are supporting QA rather than the central meal-planning story. Show theme/voice as brief interaction choices only if they strengthen the story. Keep keys, email addresses, private browser state and retrospective logs out of footage.

## Reference inheritance

Reuse Tonight Together's script authority, raw/edit separation, narration alignment, export checks and archive discipline. Replace its emulator, three participant phones, Fire TV navigation, controlled streaming provider, Amazon acknowledgement, ports and selectors. Use Qloo, Convex, OpenAI and the web interface only in the roles the CareTable implementation actually demonstrates.
